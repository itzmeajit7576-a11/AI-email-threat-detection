About Inbox Guardian

          

            An enterprise-style AI Email Threat Detection, Geolocation, and Forensic Intelligence platform engineered for modern cybersecurity operations.
          

        


        

          

            
Platform Architecture

            

              Inbox Guardian couples RFC 5322 MIME stream parsers with multi-layer heuristic threat detection engines to conduct zero-trust email triage.
            

            
    
              
  • 
                ●
                Multi-Hop Header Forensics: Deconstructs the chronological Received: chain to uncover adversary spoofing and relay rerouting.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                ●
                Cryptographic Authentication: Direct evaluation of SPF CIDR authorization, DKIM RSA-SHA256 signature hashes, and DMARC alignment.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                ●
                BGP Geolocation Correlation: Maps originating infrastructure through approximate BGP and ASN registries without relying on physical GPS assumptions.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                ●
                Safe Static Attachment Triage: Identifies hazardous container extensions (.iso, .exe, .vbs) without executing payloads on the host.
              
    •
